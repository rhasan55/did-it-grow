What would break this conclusion?

This is a simple bias calculation: subtract trend × 4.5 weeks from the estimate and interval. It is not a formal sensitivity bound, and does not resolve failed identification checks.

The measurement choice

Measure the offer’s availability across the entire eligible population. Keep existing-product revenue and service costs in the outcome. Never restrict the comparison to customers who chose to adopt.

Where this stops

Good pre-trends cannot rule out a new shock at launch. A national launch may have no unaffected donor markets. Cross-market spillovers, changed eligibility, or simultaneous pricing changes can make these estimates unusable. In that case, report the uncertainty and seek another design.
