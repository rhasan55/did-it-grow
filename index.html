Did it grow, or did customers move?

A review of a fictional offer launched in two waves. The outcome includes both the new offer and the existing product, less the added service cost.

Simulated data. 40 markets, 40 weeks, 64,000 ledger records. No Intuit data or estimate of its performance.

The gain survives the cross-product accounting.

The existing product lost some revenue, but the new offer more than covered that loss and the added service cost. I would still investigate concurrent changes and longer-term outcomes before treating this as a rollout decision.

Before using this on a real launch

Find genuinely unaffected controls. Check for changes in pricing, marketing, eligibility and customer identity across products. Passing pre-launch checks cannot rule out a new shock at rollout. If the offer is national or spills into the control markets, this design may not be available.
