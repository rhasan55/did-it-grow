# Why this question

Eric Karsten's [November 16 contractor post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7528216768436310016/) asks for launch reporting, causal analysis when randomization is unavailable, SQL pipelines, and judgment about AI-assisted analysis. His [earlier hiring post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7416923732553678848/) describes consumer business-model work on TurboTax and Credit Karma, including pricing and discounts.

Intuit [announced Credit Karma Tax on October 20, 2026](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1328/intuit-launches-credit-karma-tax-free-federal-filing-with-faster-refunds-expanding-its-daily-ai-powered-financial-assistance), with free federal filing, $15 state filing, and paid support add-ons. That makes cross-product substitution a relevant measurement question. It does not establish that Eric owns this product, that his contract concerns this launch, or that any revenue displacement occurred.

This project therefore uses a fictional offer and fictional rollout markets. It tests the accounting and comparison design without claiming to know Intuit's internal results.

Research cutoff: November 16, 2026, Eastern time. Fiscal Q1 2027 results were [scheduled for November 19](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1330/intuit-to-announce-first-quarter-fiscal-year-2027-results-on-nov-19) and are not used.
